Inside the volume

Four parts, forty chapters

A clear structure, from the science of letters to the Beautiful Names of God, so you can read the work in order or consult it by chapter.

01

Part One: Letters, Times and Squares

The science of letters, the ordering of works according to times and hours, the secrets of the Basmalah, seclusion and the etiquette of retreat, the Fatiha and Quranic openings, the Greatest Name of God, and magic squares.

02

Part Two: Disciplines and Divine Names

Disciplines and remembrances, the conditions required for operations, the Beautiful Names of God and their squares, Kaf Ha Ya Ayn Sad, the Throne Verse, and talismans.

03

Lunar Mansions and Zodiacal Signs

The judgments of the twenty-eight lunar mansions and of the twelve zodiacal signs, presented as the tradition teaches them.

04

Part Three: Names, Letters and Plants

Surat Ya Sin, the Beautiful Names of God line by line, the Arabic letters and their planetary correspondences, the Zayirjah and the ratios of letters, the Khayzabalian rules, and the properties of plants.

05

Part Four: Simiya and Invocations

The works of Simiya, the uses of the letters and their retreats, an explanation of the Most Beautiful Names, and individual invocations for all nights and times.
